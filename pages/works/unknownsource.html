Subject to the sonic address of an unknown source
May - June, 2025
Performance & Installation
Shown at Treehouse NDSM, part of the Sensory Dialogues: Attuning Our Bodies group exhibition

 

 

Subject to the sonic address of an unknown source is a site specific installation and on-going research project which imagines and attunes enclosed spaces as resonating bodies. To triggers a change in the people's listening awareness without directly addressing them, to create an instability of how we listen to art exhibitions, and how we sonically engage and participate in such spaces. The installation was setup in a metallic warehouse by NDSM; an old shipyard quarter of Amsterdam, which currently sits in a recognisable tension of artist studios and gentrified new city buildings. The walls of the warehouse were covered in parallel sets of transducer speakers (akin to a body covered in electrodes), with a stereo pair of microphones being kept inside the warehouse, and another respectively outside. A mixer sends the outside microphone signal to the transducers facing into the warehouse, and the inside microphone signal is sent to the transducers facing out.



These recordings, that go by the same name, are a series of excerpts from a three hour jam session that happened on a hot, sunny summers day in 2025. Whilst the intention of the installation was to explore subtly, we found ourselves in a lucky position to play with the sonic possibilities of this giant resonating body after the end of the exhibition. These recordings are conversations, where our movements and progressions were directly informed by us listening to the resonances and reverberations of the building. We chose each excerpt based on how well it showcases the wildly different sonic dimensions of the warehouse. Who knows what kind of histories and lives are uncovered through the resonating of a buildings cracks and leakages, let us listen closely and find out.

Made in collaboration with Xico Ribas
